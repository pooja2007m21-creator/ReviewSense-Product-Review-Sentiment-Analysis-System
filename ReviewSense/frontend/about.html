About ReviewSense

ReviewSense is an NLP application that classifies product reviews as Positive, Negative or Neutral with a trained machine-learning model, and highlights the product topics customers praise or criticise.

How It Works

Review Text
↓
Cleaning, tokenizing, stop-word removal, negation handling
↓
TF-IDF features (scikit-learn)
↓
Logistic Regression classifier
↓
Sentiment + Score + Keywords
↓
Clause-level topic insights + Statistics

Limitations

  • The model is only as good as its training data. The bundled dataset is a small synthetic demo.
  • Sarcasm, slang and non-English text are often misread.
  • Topics are found with a simple keyword list, so unusual wording is missed.
  • The star rating is an estimate from the score, not the reviewer's rating.

Technology Stack

  • HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
  • Python, Flask, SQLite
  • scikit-learn, TF-IDF
